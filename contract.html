Standard contract template

Copy this text, fill in the blanks, and have both sides sign before work begins. It is a simple starting point, not legal advice.

Freelance service agreement

  1. Parties. This agreement is between ____________ (Client) and ____________ (Freelancer).
  2. Scope of work. The Freelancer will deliver: ______________________________.
  3. Deadline. Final delivery is due on ____________. Delays caused by late feedback move the deadline by the same number of days.
  4. Fee and payment. The total fee is ₱________. The Client pays 50% before work starts and the balance within 7 days of final delivery.
  5. Revisions. The fee includes ___ rounds of revisions. Extra revisions are charged at ₱________ per hour.
  6. Ownership. The Client owns the final files after full payment. The Freelancer may show the work in a portfolio unless the Client asks otherwise in writing.
  7. Cancellation. If the Client cancels, the deposit is not refunded for work already done. If the Freelancer cancels, the unused deposit is returned.
  8. Confidentiality. Both sides keep private business information private.

Client signature: ____________________ Date: ________
