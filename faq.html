Freelancing FAQ

Select a question to see the answer.

How do I contact a freelancer?

Open their profile and use the email button. Include your project, deadline, and budget.

Does the coalition handle payment?

No. You agree on payment directly with the freelancer. Use the contract template to write it down.

What if I am not happy with the work?

Use the revision rounds in your contract first. If you still disagree, talk it through and adjust the scope or fee in writing.

Do I need a deposit?

Most freelancers ask for 50% upfront. This protects their time and shows you are committed.

Who owns the finished work?

Under our template, you own it after full payment. Confirm this in your contract.

How can I become a member?

Fill out the form on the join page. We review each application.
