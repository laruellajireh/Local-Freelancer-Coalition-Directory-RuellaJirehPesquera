How to hire a freelancer

Find a trusted local professional, agree on the work in writing, and pay fairly. This page walks you through it in four steps.

Browse freelancers

Hiring in four steps

  1. Define the job

    Write what you need, your deadline, and your budget in a few sentences.

  2. Pick a freelancer

    Compare profiles below and check that their services match your job.

  3. Sign a contract

    Use our contract template so both sides know the scope and price.

  4. Pay and review

    Pay the deposit, approve the work, then pay the balance on time.
